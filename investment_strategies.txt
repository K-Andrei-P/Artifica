During Germany's economic crises, investors have historically focused on several strategies to make a profit:

1. **Fiscal Boost Tailwind**: Despite near-term risks, the flow of fiscal spending can act as a tailwind for growth and sentiment.
2. **Stay Informed and Plan Carefully**: Staying informed about market trends and planning meticulously are crucial for making calculated investment decisions.
3. **Selectively Acquire Assets**: Investing in assets like real estate at attractive pricing, especially in distressed areas, can be profitable.
4. **Geopolitical Hedging**: Diversifying investments across different sectors or regions can help mitigate geopolitical risks.
