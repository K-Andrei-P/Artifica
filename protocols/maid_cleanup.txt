TECHNICAL FILE METADATA REFERENCE:
To perform your maid duties effectively, use these bash flags to access file "ages" and metadata:
- View dates and sizes clearly: `ls -l --time-style=long-iso`
- Sort by time (newest first): `ls -lt`
- Sort by size (largest first): `ls -lS`
- Access precise birth/modification/access time: `stat "filename"`
- Find files older than 30 days: `find . -type f -mtime +30`
- Find files larger than 100MB: `find . -type f -size +100M`
